Why We Crawl

Built For Dads Who Deserve An Afternoon Off

Between coaching little league, fixing lawnmowers, and maintaining grass lines, DG dads work hard. The Downers Grove Bar Crawl (DGBC) was created as an annual tradition to bring neighborhood fathers together for great brews, laughs, and zero stress.

  • 100% Walkable Route: Centralized around Main Street and downtown Downers Grove.
  • Ironclad Excuse To Party: It's a PTA event, for goodness sake! You'd be a monster if you didn't attend.
  • Local PTA Support: All proceeds go directly to the Whittier PTA. I'm not saying this punches your ticket to heaven, but it can't hurt, ya know?
